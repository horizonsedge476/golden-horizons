Care That Understands You

Culturally attuned senior and home care, open to every family in our community.

Golden Horizons was built on a simple idea: home care is better when it takes the time to understand your traditions and your family's expectations, not just your file number. That kind of care remains genuinely hard to find in Saskatoon. We built Golden Horizons to close that gap, while welcoming every client in our community with the same warmth, dignity, and respect, regardless of background.

What We Offer

Non-medical home care, built around your routine.

Every service below is delivered by a caregiver who comes to you: no facility, no waiting list, no change of address.

1

Companionship

Friendly, consistent visits that ease isolation and bring routine conversation and connection back into the day.

2

Personal Care

Respectful, dignified help with bathing, dressing, grooming, and toileting for clients who need a hand with daily routines.

3

Light Homemaking

Light housekeeping, laundry, and tidying so the home stays comfortable and safe without the physical strain.

4

Medication Reminders

Timely reminders to help clients stay on track with their own prescribed medication routine.

5

Respite Care

Scheduled relief for family caregivers, so the people who care for a loved one every day can rest, recover, and recharge.

6

Transportation & Errands

Rides to appointments, groceries, and errands run for clients who no longer drive or prefer not to.

Not offered at launch No skilled nursing: registered nurses, LPNs, wound care, or other clinical procedures are not part of our current service offering. Clients who need clinical/medical home care should ask us for a referral to an appropriate provider.

Our Story

Built on the same trust our family earned in child care.

Golden Horizons Care Services Inc. is women-owned and women-operated. Our CEO, Areej Nasir Khan, owns and leads the company day-to-day. Muhammad Nasir Khan serves as a Director, providing advisory guidance and operational support as needed, but the business is Areej's to build and run. We deliver trusted, non-medical private-pay home care to seniors and adults of any age across Saskatoon and area, extending a values-led care standard the family already built its reputation on.

Areej is completing her degree in biological sciences and volunteers as a caregiver at a local Saskatoon hospital, with plans to pursue a nursing career. She built Golden Horizons around the same hands-on standard of care she brings to her volunteer work every week: patient, attentive, and genuinely present with the people she's caring for.

Golden Horizons operates under a formal governance structure: a shareholders' agreement, defined decision-making authority, and an independent director alongside family leadership. That means the warmth of family-led care comes with the accountability of a properly run company.

Women-owned and women-operated. Areej doesn't just run Golden Horizons, she owns it. That's not a title, it's a fact of the cap table.
